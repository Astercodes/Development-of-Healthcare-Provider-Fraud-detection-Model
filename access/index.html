Verify your institution

Reference implementations are available at no cost to any verified institution. Verification confirms that you are a genuine institution of the kind these methods are built for. It is not an approval decision, and no institution is excluded by size, budget, sector, or geography.

How verification works →

What you'll need #

  • Your institution's regulatory identifier (NCUA charter number, FDIC certificate number, or equivalent, see below)
  • A work email address at your institution's own domain
  • A named contact

The form #

Fields marked * are required.

Institution

If credit union: NCUA charter number

If bank: FDIC certificate number

If government unit: confirmed via your .gov email domain, no additional ID needed

If other: briefly describe your institution and its regulatory identifier, if any

Contact
Request

What happens next #

Your submission is checked against public regulatory records. Most requests are verified within [X business days]. You'll receive access by email once confirmed. No further steps required on your end.

If your institution doesn't have a charter or certificate number that maps cleanly to these categories, this is common for smaller state units, say so in the form. We'll verify by another means rather than turn you away.

What we never ask for

We do not accept, and you should never send us, any claims data, credit records, protected health information, or other production data. Verification concerns your institution's identity only.
