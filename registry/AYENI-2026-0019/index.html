Healthcare Provider Fraud Detection

Description #

An expanded methodology paper behind provider level claims anomaly detection: a supervised learning study comparing Random Forest, Support Vector Machine, and K Nearest Neighbors classifiers on structured Medicare claims data, at a fraud prevalence of about 9 percent. The best configuration, a Random Forest with a depth of 25 and 1,000 estimators, reached 86.88 percent accuracy, an F1 score of 0.8187, and an AUC ROC of 0.94. This version presents the full study as a complete paper: end to end data preprocessing, clinically informed feature engineering including chronic condition indexing and derived survival status, deployment considerations for a programme integrity unit, and the regulatory framework a detector operates inside, the False Claims Act, the Anti Kickback Statute, the Stark Law, and HIPAA.
